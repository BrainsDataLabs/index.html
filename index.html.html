ZeroAssert Engine

ZeroAssert utilizes zero-knowledge SNARK proofs combined with official government identity signatures (via the Anon Aadhaar framework).

Zero Data Leakage: Verify citizenship or identity parameters without transmitting Aadhaar numbers or personal details.
Instant Verification: Sub-second cryptographic proof generation and consensus validation.
Sovereign Identity: Users maintain 100% control over their digital credentials.
